Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026.

This policy covers this website and the personal tools and apps made by its owner. They are built for the owner's own use.

This website

It is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and collects no information. GitHub may log standard server requests, as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

The tools and apps

  • They are used by the owner, on the owner's own accounts and computers. They are not offered to the public and have no users other than the owner.
  • Some of them sign in to the owner's own Google account to read or manage the owner's own data, such as the statistics of the owner's own YouTube channels. The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer.
  • No data about anyone else is collected, stored, sold or shared. Nothing is sent to third parties.
  • Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's third-party access settings, which removes the tools' permission.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
